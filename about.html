The Meaning of Amen+

The "+" represents the divine addition in every believer's life—the extra blessing, the added favor, the upgrade that comes with walking in purpose. We believe faith isn't just what you wear on Sunday; it's a lifestyle.

Amen+ was born at the intersection of faith and fashion, with a vision to create apparel that speaks to the soul while making a style statement. Founded in Ghana, our brand represents excellence, intentionality, and spiritual significance.

Every piece in our Crossroads Collection is more than clothing—it's a wearable declaration, a conversation starter, a modern-day message board for the Gospel.

You don't have to preach to be heard. Sometimes, what you wear says it all.

Our Vision

To become Africa's leading faith-inspired lifestyle brand, creating apparel that empowers believers to wear their identity with confidence while making a tangible impact in our communities.
